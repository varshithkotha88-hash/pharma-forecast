3. Methodology

Technique: Damped-trend Holt Exponential Smoothing (double exponential smoothing with a damped slope) was applied to each stock's daily closing-price series individually. This method is well-suited to short, moderately trending series: it weights recent observations more heavily than an ARIMA model would on so few data points, and the damping term prevents the trend component from extrapolating unrealistically far over a 21-trading-day (~1 month) horizon.

Validation: Each series was split into a training set and a held-out test window (the last 5–8 trading days depending on series length). The model was fit on the training portion only, then used to forecast the test window; RMSE, MAE and MAPE were computed against the actual held-out prices. The model was then refit on the complete series to generate the live 1-month-ahead forecast reported above.

Indirect variables considered qualitatively: US FDA facility inspection outcomes (a recurring theme across Sun Pharma, Dr Reddy's, Lupin and Cipla's news flow), INR/USD movement affecting export revenue, and sector-wide sentiment on the Nifty Pharma / Nifty Healthcare index. These were not fed numerically into the model (a univariate approach was used for tractability) but are noted as directional risk factors that could cause actual prices to deviate from the statistical baseline.

4. Summary of Work

This project forecasts the 1-month price trajectory of six major Indian pharmaceutical stocks — Sun Pharmaceutical Industries, Cipla, Dr Reddy's Laboratories, Lupin, Divi's Laboratories and Torrent Pharmaceuticals — using damped-trend Holt Exponential Smoothing fitted independently to each company's daily closing-price series (sourced from IIFL Capital). Each model was validated on a held-out window of its most recent trading days, achieving MAPE values ranging from 0.54% (Lupin) to 1.88% (Divi's Labs) — all comfortably under 2%, indicating strong short-horizon accuracy across the sector. Forecast direction was mixed: Sun Pharma (+0.51%), Dr Reddy's (+0.17%) and Torrent Pharmaceuticals (+0.26%) are projected mildly higher over the next month, while Cipla (-2.30%), Lupin (-0.14%) and Divi's Labs (-0.19%) are projected mildly lower or flat. No company shows a forecast move beyond ±2.5%, suggesting the sector as a whole is expected to trade in a fairly narrow range absent a major catalyst (an FDA inspection outcome, a large licensing deal, or a currency shock). Cipla's steady downward slope over its most recent three weeks is the most pronounced trend of the six and is the one series to watch most closely. Key limitation: these are univariate statistical baselines and do not incorporate fundamental or event-driven indirect variables numerically.
